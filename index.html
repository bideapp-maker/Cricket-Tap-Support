FAQ

How does scoring work?
Pick Single, Double, or Triple at the top of the dart pad, then tap the number you hit. Slash = 1 mark, X = 2 marks, ⊗ = closed (3 marks). Three darts ends your turn automatically.

What's the difference between the variants?
Standard Cricket — close 15-20 + Bull, outscore everyone.
Cut-Throat — extra hits add points to opponents instead. Low score wins.
No-Score — pure race to close all seven targets.
Wild Card — Standard, plus three random number targets each game.

How do I show the scoreboard on a TV?
Start AirPlay from Control Center while a game is in progress, or plug in an HDMI adapter. The big landscape scoreboard takes over the external display while you keep the dart pad on your phone.

Where is my data stored?
Entirely on your device. Cricket Tap has no accounts, no network calls, and no analytics. Nothing is collected, ever.

Can I undo a dart?
Yes — the curved arrow in the top-right corner of the game screen rewinds one dart at a time, across turn boundaries.

Why don't I get points for hitting a closed number?
In cricket, you only score on a target you've closed while at least one opponent has not closed it. Once everyone closes a target, it's dead.

Privacy

Cricket Tap does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. There are no accounts, no logins, no analytics, and no network calls of any kind. Game history and player stats live only on your device.
