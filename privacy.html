CRICKET TAP

Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 19, 2026

Short version: Cricket Tap collects nothing, sends nothing, and stores nothing outside your device. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads, and no third parties.

What we collect

Nothing. Cricket Tap does not collect, store, transmit, sell, share, or process any personal data. The app has no user accounts and no login system.

What stays on your device

The app saves your in-progress game and completed game history locally on your device so you can resume games and view your stats. This data lives in the app's private storage area and is never transmitted off your device. It is automatically removed when you delete the app.

Network access

Cricket Tap makes no network requests of any kind. The app does not connect to any servers — ours or anyone else's. It functions identically online and offline.

Third-party services

None. Cricket Tap contains no analytics SDKs, advertising frameworks, crash reporters, or any other third-party libraries that collect or transmit data.

External display (AirPlay)

If you mirror Cricket Tap to a TV via AirPlay or a wired connection, your iOS device handles that connection directly. No data is sent through any intermediate server.

Children's privacy

Cricket Tap is rated 4+ and contains no objectionable content. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it is fully compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar regulations worldwide.

Your rights

Because we do not collect or store your data on any server, there is nothing for us to access, modify, export, or delete on your behalf. Deleting the app removes all locally-stored data from your device. If you'd like a copy of this policy for your records, simply save this page.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will appear at this URL with a new "Last updated" date. Since Cricket Tap collects no data, any future change would only narrow what we don't collect — not expand it.
